Rose · What she remembers

From a conversation to a memory.

No forms. No app to configure. Someone just talks to Rose, and she builds grounded memory of the people who matter. Here's how it happens, and what it produces.

1 · The conversation

R
Rose
in the room · listening
Rose, Emily called today. She's bringing the grandkids next weekend.
Husband · 10:24 AM
That's wonderful. Lily and Sam? Susan always lights up when they visit. I'll remember.
Rose · 10:24 AM
✓ updated Emily's card: "visiting next weekend with Lily & Sam"
Yes. Thank you for keeping track of all of it.
Husband · 10:25 AM
Always. Would you like me to remind Susan in the morning that they're coming?
Rose · 10:25 AM

Every fact Rose stores carries who said it and when. She never invents. If she doesn't know, she says so.

2 · What it produces: Susan's people

Each card is one person in Susan's world. Rose only says what's written here, built from what you've told her, in your own words. Where she's unsure, she says so. What she's still learning, she'll gently ask you.
