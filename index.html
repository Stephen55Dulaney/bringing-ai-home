The orthogonal bet

The hyperscalers — Codex, Claude Desktop, the Copilots — are racing for the same target: the knowledge worker at their desk. Enormous, and crowded.

We point the other way. Not the AI on the desk — the AI in the room. Devices that live in the home and serve the people who live there. Not your coworker. Members of the household.

The test for everything we build

  1. Does it live in someone's home? Physically, daily, persistently — not in a tab.
  2. Does it serve the people who live there? Not their employer. The family.
  3. Does it run on our substrate? Embodied, persistent, local-first, sub-$200 hardware.

Three yeses, it's in. Any no, it's a side project — no matter how technically interesting.
