Rose · Bringing AI Home

From a conversation to a memory.

No forms. No app to configure. Someone just talks to Rose — and she builds grounded, source-attributed memory of the people who matter. Here's how it happens, and what it produces.

Every fact Rose stores carries who said it and when. She never invents — if she doesn't know, she says so.
